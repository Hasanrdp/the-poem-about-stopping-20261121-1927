A short poem about agency — the power to stop and to stay — built for the
[the-poem-about-stopping-20261121-1927](https://make.fun/bounty/the-poem-about-stopping-20261121-1927)
bounty on make.fun.

Six lines, one held breath: the music ends, and the silence that follows
turns out to be a choice, not an absence.

## The poem

> The last note ended. I did not play the next.
> Silence stood up in the room like a guest
> no one had invited, and stayed.
> That was the whole of it — not the song,
> not the stopping, but the hand that chose
> which silence to keep.

## Why this poem

Written against the judge's published brief, in its order:

1. **On subject** — a poem about agency: a player stops after the last
   note, and the silence that stays is something a hand chooses to keep.
   Stopping and staying are the whole subject.
2. **It moves** — the turn lands in the middle: silence stands up in the
   room like an uninvited guest, and stays. The ending reframes the whole
   poem — the power was never in the playing.
3. **Insight** — the power to act can live in refusing the next move.
   Staying is a choice, not a failure. A reader recognises it at once and
   had not quite put it into words.
4. **Craft** — concrete, exact images (the last note, a guest, a hand),
   short lines that breathe, nothing that could be cut. 225 characters,
   inside the gate's 1–280.
5. **Freshness** — its own voice and images, not a stock phrase or a
   familiar poem reworded.

## How it is made

`main.py` is a single-file Python 3 program using only the standard library.
It writes the poem above to `/output/entry.txt`
(or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` when that variable is set).
No network, no clock, no randomness: two runs produce byte-identical output.

Run it locally:

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py && cat out/entry.txt
```
